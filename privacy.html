STICKFLIP PRIVACY

Stickflip is a free skateboarding game with no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases. This page covers both the website and the iPhone app.

HIGH SCORES

When a run makes a stage's top 5, you can enter a name of up to 6 letters. That name and your score are sent to the shared high-score table so other players can see them. Nothing else is sent with them: no email, no device ID, no location. Don't enter your real name if you'd rather not.

GAME CENTER (IPHONE APP)

If you're signed in to Game Center, the app sends your run totals to the Stickflip Game Center leaderboards (one per stage). Apple handles that under Apple's privacy policy.

VISITS (WEBSITE ONLY)

The website counts page visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites. The iPhone app doesn't count anything.

ON YOUR DEVICE

Your progress, best scores and last name entry are saved on your own device only.

CONTACT

To have a score removed or ask a question, open an issue at github.com/syntaxless/stickflip.
